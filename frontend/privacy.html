Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 25, 2026

Xvond provides artificial-intelligence agents, business automation, and customer communication services. This policy explains how information is handled when businesses and their customers use Xvond services, including WhatsApp integrations.

Information we process

Depending on the service configuration, we may process business account details, authorized user details, customer contact identifiers, messages sent to a connected business, conversation metadata, service requests, bookings, orders, and technical logs required for security and reliability.

How information is used

Information is used only to provide configured services, route and answer customer communications, operate requested automations, maintain security, diagnose failures, measure usage, and comply with applicable legal obligations. Xvond does not sell personal information.

WhatsApp and third-party services

When a business connects WhatsApp, relevant messages and identifiers are processed to provide the requested customer-service automation. WhatsApp and Meta process information under their own terms and privacy policies. Other AI or infrastructure providers may process limited information solely to deliver the configured service.

Sharing and processors

Information may be shared with service providers only where necessary to operate Xvond, such as hosting, databases, messaging platforms, email delivery, and AI model providers. Access is restricted to the purpose for which the service was configured.

Retention and security

Information is retained only for as long as required to provide the service, meet contractual or legal requirements, and resolve security or billing matters. Xvond applies access controls, encrypted secret storage, tenant isolation, logging, and other reasonable technical safeguards.

Your choices and deletion requests

Businesses may request access, correction, export, or deletion of information associated with their account. WhatsApp users may also ask the business they contacted to correct or delete conversation data. To request deletion, email support@xvond.com with the account or phone identifier involved. We may request verification before completing a request.

Children

Xvond business services are not directed to children, and we do not knowingly offer accounts to children.

Changes

We may update this policy as services or legal requirements change. The effective date above identifies the latest version.

Contact

Questions and privacy requests: support@xvond.com.
